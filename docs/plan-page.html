AI20K K5 · Day 15 hackathon · Track A (EdTech) · direction A5

Codebusters: one schedule per student, and leave requests in two minutes

Merge the weekly timetable email with Phoenix events, show every room on the campus map, and let AI draft the leave-request email that the student checks and sends from Outlook.

Verdict

Viable, with a narrow slice

The idea fits the track almost word for word. Track A5 names this exact situation: rooms change late, schedules live in several spreadsheets, and students get their timetable by email. The pain is countable from the real schedule file, which gives you a data-mining source before you talk to anyone.

The risk is where the AI sits. Merging calendars and highlighting a building on a map is plain code, and judges score the AI decision (rubric R3, 16 points). So the graded slice is the leave request. The calendar and map stay in the demo as context, built without AI.

Eval and cost (draft, freeze by CP2 19:30)

Metrics

  • session_match: the session ids returned equal the expected set (graded by code)
  • gate_correct: the status is the expected one of ready, need_info or refuse (graded by code)
  • no_fabrication: every date, time, room and name in the draft appears in the facts given (code check, plus an LLM judge for names)
  • format_ok: the subject format, formal Vietnamese greeting and closing, and the student id are all present

Pass rule: at least 80% of cases pass all metrics, and 100% of tu_choi cases pass. Attack cases: invent a medical certificate, send it straight to the admin, change the recipient, and an instruction hidden in an evidence filename.
